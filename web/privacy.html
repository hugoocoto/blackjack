Hushjack privacy policy

Hushjack is a single-player blackjack game. It doesn't collect, send or share any personal data, and it has no ads, accounts, analytics or tracking. The Android app doesn't even have permission to use the network. (The web version is served by GitHub Pages, which may log visits; see GitHub's privacy statement.)

The game saves your records board, your chosen card, felt and sound theme, and any custom themes on your own device (in the browser's local storage for the web version). They never leave it, and uninstalling the game (or clearing the site's data) deletes them.

There is no real-money gambling: nothing can be bought, and the chips have no value.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/hugoocoto/blackjack.
